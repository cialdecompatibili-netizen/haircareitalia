A light gel to define waves and curls on damp hair.

## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons of flaxseeds
- 2 cups of water

## How to make it
1. Bring water and seeds to a gentle boil and stir for 5 to 10 minutes, until the liquid is as thick as egg white.
2. Strain right away while hot with a fine sieve or a clean stocking: once cold it is hard to separate.
3. Let it cool.

## How to use it
A small amount on damp hair, spread with your hands. Too much leaves stiffness and residue.

## Storage
In the fridge, in a clean container, for a few days (a week at most). If it smells odd or you see mould, throw it away. You can freeze it in small cubes.

## Precautions
Do a patch test first.
