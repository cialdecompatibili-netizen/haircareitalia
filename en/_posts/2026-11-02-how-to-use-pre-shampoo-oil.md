A pre-shampoo oil is applied before washing, to protect the lengths while you wash.

## How to use it
1. Warm a few drops between your hands.
2. Apply to dry or slightly damp lengths, from mid-lengths to the ends.
3. Leave on for 30 minutes to an hour.
4. Shampoo straight onto dry hair, massaging to emulsify the oil, then rinse. A second wash may be needed.

## How much
Start small. On fine hair too much weighs it down; on thick or curly hair you can increase gradually.

## Precautions
Skip the scalp if it is oily or irritated. Do the [patch test](/en/blog/hair-care/the-patch-test/) first.
