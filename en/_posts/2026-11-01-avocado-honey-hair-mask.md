A mask for dry lengths. It takes two minutes to make and is used right away.

## Ingredients (one application)
- half a ripe avocado
- 1 tablespoon of honey
- 1 tablespoon of extra virgin olive oil

## How to make it
1. Mash the avocado with a fork until there are no lumps: the smoother it is, the easier it rinses out.
2. Add the honey and oil and stir.
3. Apply to damp hair, from the lengths to the ends. Skip the roots if your scalp tends to be oily.
4. Leave on for 20 minutes, rinse with lukewarm water and shampoo.

## Storage
It does not keep: make only what you need and throw away the rest.

## Precautions
Do a patch test first (see [Before you start](/en/blog/safety/before-you-start-patch-test-and-storage/)) and skip the recipe if you are allergic to any ingredient. It does not repair damaged hair: it may help make it softer and easier to comb.
