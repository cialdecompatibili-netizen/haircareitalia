Natural does not mean risk-free: even everyday foods can irritate skin, and fresh preparations spoil quickly.

## The patch test
1. Apply a small amount of the preparation to your forearm or the inside of your elbow.
2. Wait 24 hours without washing the area.
3. If redness, itching or burning appears, wash with water right away and do not use the recipe.

## Storage
- Wash your hands, bowls and tools well before you start.
- Make only what you need. With water or fresh fruit, use it right away.
- Leftovers go in the fridge, in a clean container, with the date written on it.
- Smell, colour or look changed? Throw it away. See mould? Throw it away.

## What not to do
- Do not add essential oils at random: they are concentrated and can irritate.
- Do not apply preparations to a scalp with wounds or irritation.
- Do not use the recipes instead of seeing a doctor if you have dermatitis, persistent dandruff or sudden hair loss.

These recipes are for personal use. We are not hairdressers or dermatologists: when in doubt, ask a professional.
