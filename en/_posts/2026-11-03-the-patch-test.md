Natural does not mean risk-free: even common ingredients can irritate skin. A patch test takes two minutes and can spare you a problem.

## How to do it
1. Apply a small amount of product to your forearm or the inside of your elbow.
2. Wait 24 hours without washing the area.
3. If redness, itching or burning appears, wash with water right away and do not use the product.

## What not to do
- Do not apply products to a scalp with wounds or irritation.
- Do not use a product instead of seeing a doctor if you have dermatitis, persistent dandruff or sudden hair loss.

We are not hairdressers or dermatologists: when in doubt, ask a professional.
