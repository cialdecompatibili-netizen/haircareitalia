A traditional two-ingredient rinse, to use after shampoo.

## Ingredients
- half a cup of rice (any type)
- 2 cups of water

## How to make it
1. Rinse the rice quickly and drain.
2. Soak it in the water for 30 minutes.
3. Strain: the cloudy liquid is the rice water.
4. After shampooing, pour it over your hair, massage, leave 2 or 3 minutes and rinse.

## Storage
In the fridge, in a clean container, for one or two days at most. If the smell turns sour and strong or the look changes, throw it away.

## What to expect
Claims that it makes hair grow have no solid evidence: think of it as a rinse some people find pleasant. If your hair feels stiff, shorten the time or use it less often.
