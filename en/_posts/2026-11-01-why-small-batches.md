We make oils, serums and masks in small batches. It is not a trend: it is the simplest way to keep control of what goes into every jar.

## Few ingredients
Every product has a short list, written on the label. If an ingredient is not needed, we do not add it.

## Limited quantities
Making less at a time means less time on the shelf and more attention to every batch.

## Clear instructions
Every product tells you how to use it, in what amount and when to avoid it.

## What we do not promise
An oil or a mask can make hair softer and easier to comb. They do not repair split ends or make hair grow back, and we will never say they do.
