An oil applied before shampoo, to protect the lengths while you wash.

## Ingredients
- 1 or 2 tablespoons of virgin coconut oil (cooking oil is fine)

## How to use it
1. Warm the oil between your hands until it turns liquid.
2. Apply to dry or slightly damp lengths, from mid-lengths to the ends.
3. Leave on for 30 minutes to an hour.
4. Shampoo straight onto dry hair, massaging to emulsify the oil, then rinse. A second wash may be needed.

## Storage
Pure oil keeps as stated on its packaging, away from heat. Do not mix it with water or other fresh ingredients.

## Precautions
Skip the scalp if it is oily or irritated. On fine hair use less oil: it tends to weigh hair down. Do a patch test first.
